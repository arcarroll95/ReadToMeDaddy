Open something to read

  1. Open a PDF or paste text.
  2. Press Prepare and give it a minute or two to get ahead.
  3. Press Play. Click any sentence to jump there.

Everything runs on this computer. Your files never leave it. The first time, the voice downloads once (about 330 MB). If you're on UTMB Wi-Fi, use Guest Wi-Fi for that first download.

Keys: Space play/pause · ← → sentence back/forward · ↑ ↓ speed · P prepare
