ReadToMe voice bake-off

Every voice reads the same medical passage after the pronunciation pipeline has cleaned it. The voices run on your own computer, so nothing you test leaves it. The first load downloads the voice model (about 90–330 MB) once, and later visits reuse it.

How to run it (about 15 minutes, headphones on)

  1. Click Load voices. Wait for the status to say ready.
  2. For each voice, click Generate. That times how fast your computer makes the audio.
  3. Listen at 2× and 3.5×. Rate each voice 1–5 and jot a note if something sounds off.
  4. Do it on both the Surface and the Alienware if you can. Then click Copy results and paste them to Claude.
